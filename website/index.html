FFOIE

Freaking Fast Open Interactive Environment — a Quake-style FPS engine prototype in Rust.

Two product non-negotiables: cold start under 2–5 seconds on every target platform, and Defrag-grade movement feel — raw mouse input, fixed-timestep simulation, classic Quake PM_Accelerate / PM_AirAccelerate / PM_Friction so real strafe-jumping just works.

This is an engine prototype, not a game yet — no weapons, enemies, levels, AI, audio, or netcode. The bones (input, render, physics, asset pipeline, UI, deploy) are the focus.
